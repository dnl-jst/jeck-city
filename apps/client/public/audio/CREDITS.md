# Audio credits

All recordings are released under **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero / public domain dedication,
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>); the licence was checked on each source page.
Attribution is not required but given here. Files were cut from the sources' HQ previews, converted to
48 kHz Ogg Vorbis (q5; mono effects, stereo ambience), trimmed, faded and peak-normalised to −3 dBFS;
loops were made seamless with a short tail-into-head crossfade (sample-continuous seam).

## Announcements

`stops/*.ogg` (the trains' "Nächster Halt" and the station names) are synthesised locally by
`scripts/make-announcements.mjs` with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (MIT) and its German voice
`de_DE-ramona-low`, trained on the [M-AILABS Speech Dataset](https://github.com/imdatceleste/m-ailabs-dataset)
(BSD-style licence: use in any form including commercial; LibriVox recordings in the public domain).
